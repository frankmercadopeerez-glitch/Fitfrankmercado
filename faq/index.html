Who is Strength on Any Schedule for?

Strength on Any Schedule is self-guided general fitness education for healthy adults age 18 or older who want a strength routine that can adapt to early, late, split, standard, or rotating work schedules.

Is this personal coaching or a personalized diet?

No. Strength on Any Schedule is a digital educational product. It does not include real-time coaching, diagnosis, rehabilitation, calorie prescriptions, or medical nutrition therapy.

What equipment do I need?

The program includes bodyweight, dumbbell, and basic-gym substitutions. A stable chair or bench and a clear exercise area are useful; some options require dumbbells or gym equipment.

Does the program guarantee weight loss or muscle gain?

No. No specific body, performance, weight, or health result is guaranteed. Results vary with baseline, adherence, sleep, food intake, health, genetics, and other factors.
