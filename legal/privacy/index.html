Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Frank Mercado Fit may receive, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Contact: Frank Mercado, Cartagena, Colombia · frankmercadopeerez@gmail.com

1. Information you choose to provide

If you email Frank Mercado Fit, request a free resource, or purchase a product, you may provide information such as your name, email address, message, billing country, and order details. Do not send medical records, diagnoses, government identification, or other sensitive information by email.

2. Checkout and delivery

Free and paid digital products are delivered through Payhip. Payhip and the payment methods offered through its checkout process information needed to complete the transaction, prevent fraud, deliver files, handle tax obligations, and provide support. Frank Mercado Fit does not receive or store full card numbers. Review Payhip's and the selected payment provider's policies before checkout.

3. BMI and schedule tools

The BMI calculator and schedule planner run in your browser. The values you type into those tools are not submitted to Frank Mercado Fit, saved in a database, or used to create a profile. Avoid using a shared device if you do not want another user to see information left on screen.

4. Hosting and technical data

The hosting provider may automatically process limited technical data—such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamp, and security events—to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and maintain reliability. Frank Mercado Fit does not currently place advertising or analytics cookies on these pages.

5. Email

Transactional messages may be sent to deliver a product or answer a request. Marketing messages are sent only when you choose to receive them. You may unsubscribe using the link in a marketing email or by contacting the address above. Transactional messages about an order may still be necessary.

6. How information is used

  • deliver requested digital files and process orders;
  • answer support or pre-sale questions;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized distribution;
  • meet tax, accounting, legal, and consumer-protection obligations;
  • send optional updates when you have consented.

7. Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers needed for hosting, checkout, payment, digital delivery, email, security, professional advice, or legal compliance. Personal information is not sold.

8. Retention and security

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, legal obligations, disputes, and enforcement. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information held directly by Frank Mercado Fit, subject to legal retention duties. You may also object to or withdraw consent for optional marketing. For data controlled by Payhip or a payment provider, contact that provider directly.

10. Children

This site and its products are intended for adults age 18 or older. The site does not knowingly collect information from children. The BMI tool is specifically for adults age 20 or older.

11. International use and changes

The service is operated from Colombia and may use providers in other countries. Applicable mandatory privacy rights continue to apply. Material changes will be posted here with a new update date.
