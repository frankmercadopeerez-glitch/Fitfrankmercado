Digital purchase policy

This policy explains checkout, language selection, delivery, and support for Frank Mercado Fit digital products.

Checkout and language

Payhip processes checkout and digital delivery. Select English or Spanish before paying; the order delivers only the PDF assigned to the selected version.

Final digital purchases

Because access to the downloadable file is provided after payment, completed digital purchases are final except where applicable law requires a remedy. Nothing here limits rights that legally cannot be waived.

Delivery problems

If a download link fails, the wrong language was delivered, or a file is corrupted, email frankmercadopeerez@gmail.com with the order email, product, language, and approximate purchase date. Do not send full card numbers, passwords, or identity documents. A functional link or correct file will be provided when the order can be verified.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

Report duplicate or incorrect charges promptly through the support address and the payment provider shown on the order receipt. Mandatory payment-provider and consumer-protection procedures remain available.

Free products

Free downloads have no purchase price, but support is available for access and file problems.
