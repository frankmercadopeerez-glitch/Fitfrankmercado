Refund policy

14-day request window

You may request a refund for a paid Frank Mercado Fit digital product within 14 calendar days of the purchase date.

How to request a refund

Email frankmercadopeerez@gmail.com with:

  • the email address used for the order;
  • the product name and approximate purchase date;
  • a short reason for the request, so product issues can be understood and corrected.

Do not email full card numbers, passwords, or identification documents.

What happens next

Acknowledgment is normally sent within five business days. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. The payment provider controls final posting time. Product access may be revoked after a refund, and downloaded copies must no longer be used or shared.

Limits

One voluntary refund is available per customer. A request may be declined after 14 days, for repeated purchases and refunds, fraud, chargeback abuse, or evidence that the files were redistributed. This does not limit mandatory consumer rights or remedies for misdescription, duplicate charges, or defective delivery.

Technical problems

If a download link fails or a file is corrupted, contact support first. A working link or replacement file will be provided when possible. If the product cannot be delivered as described, an appropriate refund or remedy will be offered.

Free products

Free downloads have no payment to refund. Support is still available for access problems.

This policy covers digital products sold directly under Frank Mercado Fit. A marketplace or payment provider may also apply its own mandatory dispute procedures.
