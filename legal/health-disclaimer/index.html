Health and exercise disclaimer

Not medical care.

Frank Mercado Fit provides general educational information. It does not diagnose, prevent, treat, or cure a condition and does not replace a physician, registered dietitian, physical therapist, licensed mental-health professional, or other qualified provider.

Exercise involves risk

Physical activity can involve muscle soreness, falls, equipment failure, aggravation of an existing condition, and serious injury. Choose a safe area, inspect equipment, follow manufacturer instructions, use conservative effort, and stop when technique cannot be maintained.

When to seek professional clearance

Consult an appropriate healthcare professional before starting or changing exercise if you have a chronic condition, cardiovascular or metabolic disease, uncontrolled blood pressure, recent surgery or injury, pregnancy or postpartum considerations, significant joint or back problems, an eating disorder, unexplained symptoms, or instructions to limit activity.

Stop and seek care

Stop exercising and seek appropriate medical care for chest pain or pressure, fainting, severe or unusual shortness of breath, sudden neurological symptoms, severe pain, or any symptom that feels dangerous. In an emergency, contact local emergency services. Do not wait for an email response from this site.

Nutrition information

Food content is general education about meal structure and habits. It is not a calorie or macronutrient prescription, treatment diet, supplement prescription, or medical nutrition therapy. People with allergies, diabetes, kidney disease, gastrointestinal disease, pregnancy, eating disorders, medication interactions, or other clinical needs should work with a qualified professional.

BMI tool

The BMI calculator is for adults age 20 or older. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis, and does not directly measure body fat or account fully for muscle mass, fat distribution, age-related changes, sex-related differences, or individual health context.

No result guarantee

No specific weight, body-composition, muscle, performance, or health result is promised. Individual outcomes vary.
