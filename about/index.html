Why this exists

A system for the weeks most plans ignore.

The idea behind Frank Mercado Fit is simple: a plan that only works during a perfect week is not a useful plan.

Maritime operations and building a service business taught Frank that workdays move, weather changes plans, customers call late, and sleep sometimes needs to take priority. Strength on Any Schedule turns that reality into practical decision rules instead of shame.

The resources here are written for adults who want a structured starting point. They use conservative progression, repeatable strength movements, practical meal-building education, and clear boundaries around what a digital guide can and cannot do.

01

Honest positioning

No invented testimonials, client counts, rapid weight-loss promises, or countdown timers. Results vary and depend on many factors outside a downloadable guide.

02

Useful bilingual design

English and Spanish are included as complete resources, not a small translated section added at the end.

03

These products provide general fitness and nutrition education. They do not diagnose, treat, rehabilitate, or replace a physician, registered dietitian, licensed therapist, or other qualified professional.
