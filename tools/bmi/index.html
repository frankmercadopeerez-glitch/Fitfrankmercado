Private · local-only calculations

Two useful inputs. No account required.

Screen adult BMI and turn your available days, time, equipment, and shift pattern into a simple strength-training starting point. Your entries stay in this browser and are not stored or sent.

Tool 01

Adult BMI screening

BMI compares weight with height. It can be one part of health screening, but it is not a diagnosis and does not directly measure body fat.

Categories and age scope follow the CDC adult BMI calculator. Discuss concerns with a qualified healthcare professional.

Tool 02

Shift workout starter

Choose the time and equipment you can reliably access. The result is a general starting structure, not a personalized prescription.

Current U.S. guidance recommends muscle-strengthening activity on at least two days each week and encourages inactive adults to start small. See CDC guidance for adults.

Important limits

A number is context, not identity.

BMI does not account for muscle mass, bone density, body composition, age-related changes, sex-related differences, or fat distribution. It should be considered alongside medical history, behaviors, examination findings, and other information by a qualified professional when appropriate.

Do not use these tools for emergencies or diagnosis.

Seek urgent care for serious symptoms. Get professional clearance before exercise when a health condition, pregnancy, recent surgery, symptoms with exertion, or medical advice makes that appropriate.
