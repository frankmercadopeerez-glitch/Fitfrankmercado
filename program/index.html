A fit check before paying

This service is intentionally narrow.

It is designed for adults who want a sensible strength structure—not injury treatment, clinical nutrition or guaranteed body changes.

It may fit you if…

  • you are 18 or older
  • you want general strength or fitness programming
  • you can exercise without concerning symptoms
  • you need home, dumbbell or basic-gym options
  • you can describe your schedule and equipment accurately

Contact a qualified clinician first if…

  • you have chest pain, fainting or unexplained severe breathlessness
  • you are pregnant, postpartum or recently had surgery
  • you need rehabilitation or have been told to limit exercise
  • you need medical nutrition therapy or eating-disorder care
  • you are under 18
